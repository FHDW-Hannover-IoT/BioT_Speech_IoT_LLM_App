There are many ways to classify events, but common types include:

- **Social events** — parties, weddings, reunions, networking mixers
- **Corporate/business events** — conferences, meetings, product launches, trade shows
- **Educational events** — workshops, seminars, lectures, training sessions
- **Cultural events** — festivals, concerts, theater performances, art exhibitions
- **Sports events** — games, tournaments, marathons, races
- **Community events** — fundraisers, charity drives, local fairs, town hall meetings
- **Private/personal events** — birthdays, anniversaries, baby showers, graduations
- **Virtual events** — webinars, online conferences, live streams, remote classes
- **Religious events** — services, ceremonies, pilgrimages, celebrations
- **Political/public events** — rallies, debates, campaign events, public hearings

If you want, I can also group them by **formal vs informal**, **in-person vs online**, or **event planning purpose**.
