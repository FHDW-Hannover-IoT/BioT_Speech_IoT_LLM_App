It depends on **what device or app** you’re calibrating.

In general, calibration usually starts by:
1. Opening the device/app **Settings** or **Tools** menu
2. Finding **Calibration**, **Calibrate**, or **Sensor Setup**
3. Following the on-screen prompts
4. Keeping the device still or placing it in the required position
