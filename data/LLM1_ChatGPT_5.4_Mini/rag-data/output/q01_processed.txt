You view sensor data by running the app and letting it connect to the MQTT broker; it subscribes to the sensor topics, receives incoming sensor messages, updates the UI fields, and saves the data to the local Room database. On the UI, incoming data is shown in the accelerometer, gyro, and timestamp TextViews for the topics `Sensor/Bewegung`, `Sensor/Gyro`, and `Sensor/Zeit` respectively [Doku.pdf].
