It depends on the device or app you’re using.

Common ways to view sensor data:
- **On the device itself:** Open the **Sensors**, **Diagnostics**, or **Status** menu.
- **In a mobile app:** Connect the sensor, then check the **Dashboard**, **Live Data**, or **Telemetry** tab.
- **On a computer:** Use the manufacturer’s software or a web dashboard to view real-time and logged data.
- **Via API/serial/CSV:** Some sensors export data to a file, serial monitor, or an endpoint.
