# Types of Events

Events can be categorized in many ways:

## By Purpose
- **Business** – conferences, trade shows, meetings
- **Social** – parties, weddings, reunions
- **Educational** – seminars, workshops, lectures
- **Entertainment** – concerts, festivals, sporting events
- **Fundraising** – charity galas, benefit dinners
- **Promotional** – product launches, grand openings

## By Scale
- **Personal** – intimate gatherings
- **Small** – 10-100 people
- **Medium** – 100-1,000 people
- **Large** – 1,000+ people

## By Format
- **In-person** – physical attendees
- **Virtual** – online/digital
- **Hybrid** – combination of in-person and virtual

## By Duration
- **One-time** – single day or occasion
- **Multi-day** – conferences, festivals
- **Recurring** – annual events, weekly meetups

## By Type
- Corporate events
- Community events
- Religious/ceremonial events
- Sports events
- Cultural events
- Networking events
