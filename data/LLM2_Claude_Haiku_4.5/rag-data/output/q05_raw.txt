# To turn off your system:

**Windows:**
- Click Start menu → Power → Shut down
- Or press `Alt + F4` and select shutdown
- Or use `Win + X` → Shut down

**Mac:**
- Click Apple menu → Shut Down
- Or press `Control + Command + Power` button

**Linux:**
- Use `sudo shutdown -h now` in terminal
- Or click system menu → Power Off

**Chromebook:**
- Click system menu (bottom-right) → Power → Shutdown
