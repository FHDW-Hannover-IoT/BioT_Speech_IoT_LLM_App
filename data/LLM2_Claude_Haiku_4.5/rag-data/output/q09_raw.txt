# Types of Events

Events can be categorized in several ways:

## By Purpose
- **Corporate** - conferences, meetings, product launches
- **Social** - weddings, parties, reunions
- **Educational** - seminars, workshops, webinars
- **Entertainment** - concerts, festivals, sports
- **Charity** - fundraisers, awareness campaigns

## By Scale
- **Large** - festivals, conventions (1000+ people)
- **Medium** - conferences, galas (100-1000 people)
- **Small** - intimate gatherings (under 100 people)

## By Format
- **In-person** - physical gatherings
- **Virtual** - online/digital events
- **Hybrid** - combination of both

## By Duration
- **One-time** - single occurrence
- **Recurring** - regular happenings (annual, monthly)

## Other Categories
- **Professional** - networking, trade shows
- **Cultural** - performances, exhibitions
- **Sports** - competitions, tournaments
- **Religious/Ceremonial** - holidays, rituals
